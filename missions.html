How a mission works.

  1. Publish. I publish a mission with a brief, a deadline and the scoring criteria.
  2. Register. You register your interest (optional but helpful).
  3. Build. Work alone or form a team. Credit everyone.
  4. Submit. Send your project through the Mission Desk before the deadline.
  5. Review. Reviewers score it against the published criteria.
  6. Feedback. Scores, reasons and comments appear in the Royal Ledger.
  7. Reassess. If you think a score is wrong, request a reassessment.
  8. Record. Your achievement is added to the Ledger, including any corrections.
  9. Invitations. Outstanding builders may be invited to further opportunities. That’s an invitation, not a promise.

    Levels

      Levels are recognition for completed work. They are not a funding qualification.

      The Mission Desk.

      Everything you do on a mission starts here. You need your member key, which is the petition reference (like RAE-2026-ABC234) on your Petition Received screen. We use it to check you’ve petitioned. It is never shown publicly. Haven’t petitioned yet?

      Projects and completed work.

      Work that builders have chosen to share. Projects appear here only after I’ve reviewed them. A project being shown is not independent verification that it works or that the claims are true: it’s what the builder submitted, with the reviewer’s comment alongside.

      The Royal Ledger.

      A public record of completed missions, with every score explained. Points reward actual work. Open any builder to see exactly why points were awarded or deducted, and request a reassessment if you think something is wrong.
