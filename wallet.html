The Republic’s own currency

The Royal Wallet.

Crowns are the Republic’s points. You earn them through real work, and spend them on ads and on offers from the Supreme Leader.

The rules of the Crown: Crowns are earned by taking part: Royal Missions, helping other builders and joining in. They have no cash value, can’t be transferred, and can’t be exchanged for money. Your level depends only on Crowns you’ve earned, never on what you’ve spent.

How to earn Crowns.

  • Royal Missions: strong, honest work on a mission earns Crowns.
  • Helping other builders: reviewing a pitch, sharing a skill, making an introduction.
  • Taking part: showing up for the community and for events.

The Supreme Leader awards Crowns personally and writes the reason, so you can always see why. Crowns can’t be bought, at least for now.
