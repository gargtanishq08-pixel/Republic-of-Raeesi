What this means

  • Applying is free, and so is the mentorship.
  • ₹1,00,000 is the total initial pool being considered across all ventures.
  • I decide which ventures, if any, receive investment, and how the pool may be allocated.
  • Investment may involve equity. Illustrative terms are discussed and negotiated individually.
  • Any real investment needs proper legal documentation and the formalities that apply.
  • Mentorship never requires you to accept an investment or give up equity.

What this does not mean

  • It is not a prize, an automatic grant, or guaranteed cash.
  • It is not ₹1,00,000 for every applicant.
  • A great petition does not mean a cheque. It may mean mentorship, introductions and a push.
  • A high Royal Ledger score is not a cheque. Missions and scores can inform my decision; they never decide it automatically.
  • Nobody gets “selected” by an algorithm. I read the petitions myself.

The Supreme Founder’s Origin Story

Why the Republic was founded

When I started building ventures, I found out how hard it is to move forward without money, connections, or any idea how the legal and administrative side works.

Funding and grants came with requirements I didn’t know how to navigate. Professional guidance and incorporation meant extra costs before a business had even started.

So I started thinking about other people. A 15-year-old with a product idea. A 17-year-old experimenting with technology. An 18-year-old trying to build a business with no money to hire a developer or a professional adviser.

I want to look at the person behind the idea, not only the company they’ve already managed to create. I care about how people think, learn, lead, adapt and execute. Technology changes, markets change, business models evolve. I’m looking for people who can keep learning and building through all of it.

Money matters. But having no money should not automatically rule someone out.

That’s why the Republic offers free mentorship, a community where builders support each other, and, selectively, seed funding for promising ventures. Whether you need collaborators, a validated idea, an MVP, business fundamentals or, eventually, investment, the goal is the same: help builders move forward.

“I am not just looking for startups that already exist. I am looking for people who have the potential to create what does not exist yet.”

Raeesi, Supreme Leader

A clarification from the Supreme Leader.

Let’s establish one tiny constitutional detail before you enter the Republic.

I am your mentor. I am your potential seed investor. I am the person who might help you find your next developer, fix your business model, or stop you from spending three months building something nobody wants.

I am NOT automatically your co-founder.

I will not wake up one morning to discover that I own 40% of your startup because I answered three WhatsApp messages and gave you some advice.

You build your company. You own your execution. You make your decisions.

If, in a particular venture, we independently decide that a deeper partnership makes sense, we can discuss it separately, negotiate properly, and document everything.

Until then, the Supreme Leader shall remain in his advisory palace.

The serious version

Mentorship, advice and community membership do not automatically create an equity interest, a partnership, an employment relationship, or a co-founder arrangement. Any deeper arrangement would be a separate, voluntary, negotiated agreement that is written down properly.

The standard of the Republic.

Every serious republic needs a coat of arms, a flag and a motto it can print on things. Ours is Integritas, Innovatio, Imperium: integrity, innovation, empire.

Age, privacy and trust.

Plain answers, no fine-print theatre.

Who can apply?

Builders of roughly 14 to 25, from any country. You don’t need a company, revenue, an MVP or a pitch deck. “It’s still just an idea” is a perfectly good place to start.

What information do you collect?

Your name, email, country, age range and how you’d like to be contacted. Then your answers to the 20 questions, any links you choose to add, and any evidence you choose to attach. That’s all. Please don’t send ID documents, bank details or passwords.

How is it used, and who can see it?

Only to review petitions, talk to you, and invite you into the community. Right now, only I read them. Petitions are saved in a private Google Sheet, and uploaded files go to a private Google Drive folder, that only I can open. Google provides that storage for me. If this ever changes, this page will say so.

Will you publish my idea, files or contact details?

No. Ideas, emails, phone numbers and files are not published on this website or shared publicly.

I’m under 18. What’s different?

You can still apply. If you’re 14 to 17, the petition asks for a parent or guardian’s details and their agreement to you applying. Mentorship is free and stays separate from any investment talk. Any actual investment or contract involving someone under 18 would need a parent or guardian, proper legal paperwork, and professional legal advice before anything is agreed.

Can I ask you to show, correct or delete my petition?

Yes, at any time. Just contact me at through the way you applied, and tell me your petition reference number.
