Three ways to get involved.

Sponsor a Royal Mission or prize

Put your name on a monthly challenge, or fund its prize. Sponsors are named on the mission page. You don’t get a say in the scoring or the results.

Sponsor an event

A build marathon, a workshop, a dinner or a meetup for the community. We run the community and the programme; you help make it happen.

Tell our builders about an opportunity

Programmes, colleges, accelerators and companies can tell our builders about an intake, scholarship, internship or job. We share it with the community and members choose whether to apply. We never hand over member details.

The ground rules.

  • Independent. Sponsors never influence mission scoring, who is granted an audience, or any funding decision.
  • Honest. We only name sponsors who have agreed to be named. A listing means an organisation supports the Republic. It does not mean the Republic is partnered with, affiliated with or endorsed by them.
  • Members’ privacy. We don’t share member details. For anyone under 18, opportunities need a parent or guardian involved.
  • In writing first. Terms are agreed in writing before anything is announced.
  • Plain numbers. We don’t inflate the size of the community. Ask what it looks like today and we’ll tell you plainly.

Questions.

Is the Republic partnered with any organisation?

No. The Republic is independent. A sponsor’s listing means they support us; it does not mean we are partners, or that we are endorsed by them.

Can we recruit members directly?

You can tell our builders about your opportunity. We share it with the community and members apply themselves. We don’t hand over member lists or contact details.

What does sponsorship cost?

That’s agreed case by case, in writing. We’ll be straightforward about what we can offer in return, and we won’t promise anything we can’t deliver.

Where does my information go?

Your enquiry goes to a private spreadsheet that only the Supreme Leader can open. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else.
