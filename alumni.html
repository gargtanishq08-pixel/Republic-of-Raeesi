How to join the Alumni Body.

There’s no application fee and no shortcut. Alumni are builders who took part for real: they joined the Builders’ Network, completed Royal Missions, helped other members, and reached the upper levels of the Royal Ledger. The Supreme Leader recognises them personally.

Alumni are invited to give back: mentoring newer members, judging missions, and sharing what they learned. That is how everyone in the Republic grows.
